Hong Kong temperature heatmap

CSCE 679 Data Visualization, Assignment 1. Daily temperature readings for Hong Kong, 1997 to 2017, drawn with D3.

  • Level 1 — year/month matrix: one cell per month across all 21 years, with a toggle between the monthly maximum and minimum.
  • Level 2 — daily detail: the last 10 years, with each cell holding a line chart of that month's daily highs and lows.
